States & Capitols

Every cell starts as its own state. Drag from a cell across others to merge them into its state. Use Split mode (or right-drag / shift-drag) to cut cells loose. Hatched states have no symmetry. Switch to Mark capitol and tap a cell to place or remove a capitol dot (red = not a true capitol of its state), or tick auto-mark. Clue numbers turn green when the shortest cost to a capitol matches.
